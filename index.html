About / Practice

I am a computational designer, design engineer, and researcher.

I gain fresh perspectives by challenging how we interact with physical spaces and our perception of "constructed" and "natural" environment. I believe that the essence of technology is by no means anything technological, and should be, about the system it embodies.

My works explore the intersection of emerging technologies, data, tool building, and design with systems. My practice focuses on interoperability, spatial systems and design, spatial AI, and digital-physical workflows.
