07 / Position + method

About / Practice

I am a computational designer, architectural designer, and researcher based in Los Angeles.

My work focuses on design computation: systems thinking, environmental analysis, resilient design, parametric and algorithmic workflows, performance simulation, machine-learning applications, data visualization, and digital tool development.

I use these methods to challenge how we interact with physical spaces and how we understand constructed and natural environments as systems.
