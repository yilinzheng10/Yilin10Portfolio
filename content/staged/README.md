# Staged Drafts

This folder is for structured drafts after Codex has classified and rewritten rough inbox notes.

Staged content is still private or draft by default. Do not render staged content publicly.

Before moving an item to `content/published/`, confirm:

- The title is accurate.
- The date is real.
- The organization, role, location, collaborators, and links are verified.
- Media paths exist or external media links are correct.
- Claims are specific and not inflated.
- `status` is changed to `published`.
- `visibility` is changed to `public`.
